Sudorobo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Sudorobo (Toprak Studio) collects, uses and protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

You do not need to create an account to play Sudorobo. The app creates an anonymous player account for you. If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is backed up to the cloud and can be restored on a new device. The app may process:

  • In-game profile: the username you choose or the app assigns, avatar, frame and robot skin. These are visible to other players once you appear on the leaderboard.
  • Account information: an anonymous player ID and, if you signed in with Play Games, the user ID linked to that account. The app never sees your password.
  • Game data: leaderboard scores and, if signed in with Play Games, a progress backup (levels, chips, hints, unlocked skins, task and streak data). Other game data stays on your device.
  • Device and advertising identifiers: the Advertising ID and similar device information are processed by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide daily and overall leaderboards and leaderboard rewards
  • To back up and restore your progress
  • To show and measure ads

3. Third-Party Services

These services are subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore): player account, leaderboards and cloud backup. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Google Play Games Services: player sign-in and account linking.

4. Advertising Choices

Where required, the app asks for your consent for ads. You can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy settings.

5. Data Sharing

Your personal data is not sold or shared with third parties other than the service providers above, unless required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

Sudorobo does not specifically target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe your child has provided us personal data, contact us at the address below and it will be deleted.

7. Data Security and Retention

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure protected by security measures. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Sudorobo (developer: Toprak Studio) account and data at any time:

  1. Open Settings in Sudorobo and tap the Player ID row to copy your ID.
  2. Email toprakstudioapp@gmail.com with the subject "Sudorobo account deletion" and include your Player ID.
  3. Deletion is completed within 30 days and you will be notified.

Deleted: your player account, username and profile, leaderboard records and cloud backup.

Not deleted: advertising data processed by Google AdMob is kept under Google's own policies. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

To delete only part of your data, write to the same address with the subject "Sudorobo data deletion". Deletion cannot be undone.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be posted on this page.

10. Contact
